## Description

**Altboard** shows all your characters in one modern, dark window, so you know at a glance which alt still has things to do this week.

### What you see for each character
- **Item level**, coloured by upgrade track
- **Gold**, plus the warband bank and an account total
- **Great Vault**: three pips each for raid, dungeons and world. A **Reward!** flag appears when a vault is waiting to be opened
- **Keystone** and **Mythic+ rating**
- **Crests**, **Restored Coffer Keys** and **Valorstones**
- **Rested XP**, which keeps counting while the character is logged out (faster if you left them in an inn or city)
- When you last played them

Hover a character for lockouts with time left, currencies with weekly and season caps, professions, and the Great Vault slot by slot with reward item levels.

### Details
- Weekly data resets by itself after the weekly reset, even for characters you have not logged into since
- Click a column header to sort; right-click a character to hide or forget it
- Open it with `/alts`, the minimap button, the addon compartment, or a key binding
- No libraries, no setup: log in once on each character and it appears
- English and Spanish

Made for retail **The War Within (11.2.5)**.

## Descripción (español)

**Altboard** muestra todos tus personajes en una ventana moderna y oscura, para saber de un vistazo qué alt tiene cosas pendientes esta semana.

- **Nivel de objeto**, **oro** (con el banco de banda y el total), **Gran Cámara** con aviso de **¡Recompensa!**, **piedra angular** y **puntuación M+**, **blasones**, **llaves de arca** y **piedras de valor**, **descanso** que sigue contando sin conectarte, y cuándo jugaste por última vez
- Pasa el ratón por un personaje para ver bloqueos, monedas con topes, profesiones y la cámara en detalle
- Los datos semanales se reinician solos tras el reinicio semanal
- `/alts`, botón del minimapa, menú de addons o atajo de teclado
