# Rastro — CurseForge page

**Name:** Rastro
**Summary:** What you can still collect where you stand: missing mounts and pets in this zone, and the mounts, pets, toys and appearances each boss here can still give you.

## Description

**Rastro** ("trail") tells you what is still out there for you, wherever you are. A small pill under the minimap counts it; click it for the details.

### In the open world
- **Mounts** and **pets** you are missing whose source is this zone
- **Wild battle pets** of the zone, listed apart
- **Dungeons and raids** with an entrance in the zone: click one to see what you are missing there, by difficulty

### Inside a dungeon or raid
- The **mounts, pets, toys and appearances** your character can still collect, **grouped by boss**
- Bosses you already killed this lockout are marked

### Handy
- Click opens the mount or pet in your collections, **ctrl-click previews** it, shift-click links it
- Everything comes from the game's own data (Adventure Guide and collection journals): nothing to download or update
- Shift-drag the pill to move it; `/rastro`, the addon compartment, or a key binding open the panel
- No libraries; English and Spanish

Zone results come from the source text the game gives each mount and pet, so an item whose source does not name the zone will not show there.

Made for retail **The War Within (11.2.5)**.

## Descripción (español)

**Rastro** te dice qué te queda por conseguir allí donde estás. Una pastilla bajo el minimapa lo cuenta; púlsala para ver el detalle.

- **En el mundo:** monturas y mascotas que te faltan de la zona, mascotas salvajes aparte, y las mazmorras y bandas con entrada en la zona (púlsalas para ver lo que te falta en cada dificultad)
- **En una mazmorra o banda:** monturas, mascotas, juguetes y apariencias que aún puedes conseguir, por jefe, marcando los que ya mataste
- Clic abre tus colecciones, Ctrl+clic lo prueba, Mayús+clic lo enlaza
