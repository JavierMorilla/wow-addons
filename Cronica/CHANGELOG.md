# Crónica

## 0.1.0

First release.

- A timeline per character, grouped by day: level-ups, achievements, first victory over each boss and difficulty, Mythic+ runs, epic and legendary gear, new mounts, pets and toys, renown levels and first visits to zones, dungeons, raids and delves
- Automatic screenshots of the big moments (can be turned off)
- Import the achievements a character already earned, with their dates
- Filters, search and a character sidebar; click an achievement to open it, shift-click to link, ctrl-click gear to preview it
- English and Spanish
